YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 01

The Signal on the Headland

Inference · atmosphere · symbolism

Look for

Look for repeated checks of the horizon, descriptions of the light, weather and changes in the character’s behaviour.

Answer-ready sentences

The repeated focus on the light suggests ___, because ___. The description of ___ creates a tense atmosphere by ___. The signal may symbolise ___ because ___.

Remember

Use a precise word or action from the passage. Do not guess what the signal means without evidence.
