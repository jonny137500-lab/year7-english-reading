YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 03

A Case for the Later Start

Persuasion · claims · evaluation

Look for

Identify the writer’s main contention, supporting reasons, evidence and any counterargument. Notice emotive language, statistics and rhetorical questions.

Answer-ready sentences

The writer argues that ___. The use of ___ is intended to persuade ___ by ___. The argument is convincing / less convincing because ___.

Remember

Name the audience and explain the effect of the technique.
