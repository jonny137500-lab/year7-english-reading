YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 06

The Last Patch of Grass

Setting · imagery · competing needs

Look for

Look at contrasts such as grass and concrete, shade and heat, or quiet and noise. Identify what different characters value about the space.

Answer-ready sentences

The contrast between ___ and ___ highlights ___. The patch of grass matters to ___ because ___. The writer’s tone is ___, shown by ___.

Remember

Explain why the place matters; do not only describe its appearance.
