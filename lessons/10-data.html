When the Data Disagreed

Data reasoning · reliability · limitations

Look for

Compare how each study defines the question, selects its sample and collects results. Check averages, outliers and limitations.

Answer-ready sentences

The results differ because the studies use ___ and ___. This makes ___ stronger evidence for ___, but more data is needed to establish ___.

Remember

Do not choose the number you prefer. Evaluate how it was collected.
