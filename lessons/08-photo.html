YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 08

The Photograph That Wasn’t There

Visual literacy · reliability · ambiguity

Look for

Describe what is actually visible before inferring. Consider framing, cropping, timing, captions and what may be outside the image.

Answer-ready sentences

The photograph draws attention to ___ through ___. The absence of ___ may suggest ___, although another explanation could be ___.

Remember

A photograph can be genuine but incomplete or misleading.
