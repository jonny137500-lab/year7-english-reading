YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 02

The Creek That Disappeared

Cause and effect · evidence · viewpoint

Look for

Separate what people observe from what they think caused it. Compare dates, measurements and the views of residents, farmers or scientists.

Answer-ready sentences

The passage suggests that ___ may have contributed to the creek’s disappearance. The evidence is ___, although it does not prove ___.

Remember

A possible cause is not automatically a proven cause.
