YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 07

A Message from the Future

Speculation · plausibility · tone

Look for

Separate facts from predictions. Check whether the message fits the passage’s world and notice language that makes the narrator sound urgent or uncertain.

Answer-ready sentences

The message seems ___ because ___. The detail ___ makes the prediction more / less believable, while ___ leaves the reader uncertain about ___.

Remember

A prediction is not a fact. Acknowledge uncertainty when needed.
