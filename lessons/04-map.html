YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 04

The Map in the Attic

Mystery · foreshadowing · character

Look for

Track what the character knows before and after finding the map. Look for earlier details that become important later.

Answer-ready sentences

The map changes the character’s understanding of ___ because ___. The detail about ___ foreshadows ___ and makes the reader expect ___.

Remember

Foreshadowing hints at a later event; it does not reveal everything.
