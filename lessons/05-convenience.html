YEAR 7 ENGLISH · QUICK ANSWER GUIDE 05

The Cost of Convenience

Argument · statistics · bias

Look for

Check what each statistic measures, its units and comparison. Separate facts from opinions and notice emotionally loaded words.

Answer-ready sentences

The figure ___ shows ___. This supports the claim that ___, but it does not prove ___. The word ___ encourages readers to view ___ as ___.

Remember

Do not confuse a statistic with the writer’s opinion about it.
