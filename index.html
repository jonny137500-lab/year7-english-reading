A reliable method for any reading

  1. Read once for meaning. Identify who or what the passage is mainly about and what changes.
  2. Read again for clues. Underline precise words, actions, contrasts and details that seem important.
  3. Answer the question asked. Do not retell the whole passage when asked to infer, explain language or evaluate.
  4. Support your idea. Use a short quotation or a precise detail, then explain what it suggests.
  5. Check your reasoning. Make sure your explanation is supported rather than merely possible.
